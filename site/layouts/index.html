Status

I'm building commercial web applications in k1, and have prototyped native games and desktop apps. It's highly usable, fast, and enjoyable.

Some of the std code, and the included `modules`, are more AI-assisted than I'd like. Its an incredibly useful scaffolding technique, but ultimately in the long term, antithetical to the project. I'd love community support and help to build out the ecosystem by hand, with craft and care, and tear down the scaffolds!

k1 still has a long way to go. Considering joining me in making it even better and building software with it.
