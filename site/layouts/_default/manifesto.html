One language: data, build, code, meta. Your data files are k1. Your build script is k1. Your program is k1. Your metaprograms are k1 functions that run early and return k1 source.

You don’t choose C. It’s how you talk to your OS and to every library that matters. k1 is fanatically compatible with it, by default: structs, functions, calling convention, all of it. It plugs straight in.

Not memory-safe by default; safety is opt-in, through Fil-C. Not a Rust replacement; it is on a different axis. Not garbage-collected, though it could be.
