My goal is to build up and lift up, not tear down. I love every programming language on this list.

I'm inspired by Ken Thompson and Dennis Ritchie and C, I'm inspired by Graydon Hoare and everyone who has worked on Rust; its a marvel. I'm inspired by Bjarne Stroustrup and the breadth and influence of C++. I'm inspired by Andrew Kelley, his tenacity and vision for Zig. I'm inspired by Chris Lattner, and I think LLVM is goated, even if I can [SROA k1 28x faster than it can](https://x.com/kolemannix/status/2106466381432545651). I'm inspired by all of the incredible work that's gone into Java and the JVM over the years (it was my first language!). I'm inspired by Go; its simplicity, and its goal of loosing the bonds of the C layer, unstable syscalls be damned. I'm inspired by Python's popularity and dream of making something as beloved. I'm inspired by Clojure and Rich Hickey's elegant ideas and bold independent thinking. I'm inspired by the V8 engine squeezing water from stone, and I'm inspired by Martin Odersky's consistent pseudo-prophetic vision for where programming needs to go next.

Complexity is the enemy, programming is sublime, humans matter, and computers are amazing. I love programming languages, and programming, and all of you. PRs and corrections are more than welcome.
